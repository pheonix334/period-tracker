How it works

  1. Enter the first day of your last period. That's the one date this calculator actually needs.
  2. Set your average cycle and period length. 28-day cycles and 5-day periods are typical defaults, but adjust to match your own pattern if you know it.
  3. We estimate your next period by adding your cycle length to your last period's start date.
  4. We estimate ovulation as roughly 14 days before your next period, with a 6-day fertile window (5 days before ovulation through 1 day after) reflecting sperm and egg viability.
  5. Your calendar shows the pattern projected across the next few cycles.
Important: Ovulation estimates aren't exact — actual timing can shift by several days each cycle. This isn't a method of birth control. If you have irregular cycles, very heavy bleeding, severe pain, or think you might be pregnant, talk to a clinician.
